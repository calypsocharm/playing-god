What kind of house did they grow up in?

This is the part nobody chooses in life. Here you do. It writes the first rules into the body before day one.

A warm houseSomeone was steady. They start open, trusting a little by default, no wounds. Easiest to hurt for the first time, quickest to heal.
A cold houseNobody came. They start tight and wary with one rule already written: either "closeness leads to pain" or "weakness gets punished." The village decides which.
A house that changed with the weatherSometimes held, sometimes left. They start with "needing someone means being left." They will not ask for help even when starving.
